# Healthy Family Hub

Build a public health awareness website called "Health Awareness — A Healthy Family, A Happy Family" for a Community Service Project. The audience includes rural residents, elderly people, and people with low literacy, so simplicity and visual clarity matter more than fancy design.



CORE REQUIREMENTS:



1. LANGUAGE ACCESSIBILITY

- Add a language selector in the top navbar (English, Hindi, Telugu at minimum — I'll add more later)

- All page content should update based on selected language

- Use simple, short sentences — avoid medical jargon



2. VISUAL-FIRST DESIGN

- Every health tip must be paired with a clear, friendly illustration or icon (no adult/graphic content, family-friendly cartoon style)

- Use large, legible fonts (minimum 16px body text) and high color contrast for readability

- Icon-based navigation so people who can't read well can still recognize sections by image alone



3. HOMEPAGE

- Hero section with the tagline "A Healthy Family, A Happy Family" and a friendly illustration

- Large clickable cards for each awareness category (see below), each with an icon/image and one-line description

- A simple search bar to find topics



4. AWARENESS CATEGORIES (each as its own page/section with image + short bullet points + simple examples):

   a) Personal Hygiene — handwashing, using soap, safe drinking water, clean surroundings

   b) Mosquito & Disease Prevention — no stagnant water, covered water containers, repellents, clean drains, dengue/malaria basics

   c) Nutrition — balanced diet plate visual, eat more (fruits/veggies/whole grains/water) vs eat less (junk food/soft drinks), simple food pyramid graphic

   d) Mental & Spiritual Health — stress management, importance of rest, talking to someone, community support

   e) General Disease Awareness (e.g., HIV/AIDS, seasonal illnesses) — presented sensitively, factual, non-stigmatizing, with "myths vs facts" format

   f) Women & Family Health — basic awareness on maternal health, adolescent health (age-appropriate, non-graphic)



   For each category, include:

   - A relatable "why this matters" example or short story

   - 3-5 simple actionable tips with icons

   - A "Do's and Don'ts" visual comparison (like Eat More / Eat Less style from my poster)



5. INTERACTIVE/ENGAGING ELEMENTS

- Simple quiz or "test your knowledge" section per topic (multiple choice, very easy)

- A "Daily Health Tip" widget on homepage that rotates

- Downloadable/printable posters (PDF) for each topic that community volunteers can print and distribute



6. NAVIGATION & UX

- Sticky top navbar with icon + text labels (Home, Topics, Quiz, Resources, Contact)

- Mobile-first responsive design since most rural users will access via phone

- Large tap targets, minimal scrolling per section, avoid clutter

- Text-to-speech button (if possible) to read content aloud for non-readers



7. FOOTER

- Contact/helpline information section

- Credits: "A Community Service Project by [Your Name/College]"

- Links to trusted health resources (govt health department, WHO, etc.)



8. DESIGN STYLE

- Warm, friendly, non-clinical color palette (soft greens, blues, yellows — avoid harsh red/white hospital look except for warning icons)

- Rounded cards, soft shadows, hand-drawn/cartoon-style illustrations to feel approachable rather than intimidating

- No stock photos of hospitals/needles/graphic medical imagery — keep it welcoming



Please generate the homepage first with navigation and hero section, then I'll ask for individual topic pages one at a time.
